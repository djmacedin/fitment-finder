Before buying or fitting wheels

Verify the exact VIN, engine/trim, front and rear brake clearance, wheel diameter and width, offset, centre-bore/hub ring suitability, correct bolt/nut thread and seat, tyre load/speed rating, TPMS and applicable manufacturer specifications. Never assume matching PCD means a safe fit.
